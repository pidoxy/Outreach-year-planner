      Planning notes & approval process

      Retreats are planned for January and July. Programme dates are placeholders; edit the programme in Programmes and related deadlines move with it. Approvals follow the national chain: Unit Head and Team Lead, Management Committee (weekly), Youth Head, then PQAC. Programmes, tasks and progress are publicly viewable. Sign-in is required to make changes. Administrators can download and restore planner backups; account credentials are managed separately.
